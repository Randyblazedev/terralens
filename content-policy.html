Content Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

These rules keep TerraLens useful, safe and honest for everyone who explores and shares places.

The short version

TerraLens is for real places, honest information and photos you took. Be kind, be accurate and protect people and places. We review new uploads, and we remove content that breaks these rules.

What belongs on TerraLens

  • Your own photos of real places that are worth seeing, taken lawfully.
  • Honest descriptions, with accurate names, locations and practical details such as season, access and costs where you know them.
  • Helpful, respectful comments with tips and experiences.

What is not allowed

  • Photos or text you do not have the right to share, including images copied from other websites, social media or other photographers.
  • Nudity, sexual content, graphic violence, gore, or anything that sexualises or endangers children. We report child exploitation to the authorities.
  • Hate, harassment, threats, bullying or discrimination against people or groups.
  • Illegal activity or content that encourages it, including trespass, poaching, looting, dangerous stunts or harming wildlife or heritage sites.
  • Spam, scams, fake places, misleading edits or photos that misrepresent a place, and fake or repeated comments.
  • Advertising or promotional links that are not clearly disclosed. Paid or sponsored content must be labelled.
  • Other people's private information (such as home addresses or phone numbers), or photos that identify private individuals without their permission.
  • Impersonating someone, or pretending to be a verified or official source.
  • Malware, attempts to break the site, or automated scraping.

Protecting people

Do not upload photos where a person, especially a child, is the main subject unless you have their (or a guardian's) permission. Crowd and landscape photos where people are incidental are fine. Remove location details for private homes. If you are in a photo and want it removed, email our contact email.

Protecting places

Some places are fragile or sensitive: nesting or breeding sites, protected habitats, burial or sacred sites, and unmarked archaeological sites. Do not publish exact locations that could lead to damage. Respect closures, permits, local customs and private land. Leave no trace.

Photos and AI-made images

Photos should be real pictures of the place you name. Light editing such as cropping and colour correction is fine. Do not upload AI-generated or heavily composited images as if they were real photos of a place.

How moderation works

  • New uploads from new contributors are reviewed before they appear.
  • Anyone signed in can report a place or comment. Moderators review reports.
  • Depending on the problem we may approve, ask for changes, send a place back to review, hide it, remove it, limit an account or close it. Serious or repeated breaches can lead to an immediate ban.
  • You can edit a place you uploaded for one hour after uploading it. Later changes need a moderator.

The verified badge

A gold badge marks contributors we trust. It is usually considered after about 100 approved photos across at least 10 places, with no upheld reports in the last 90 days. We decide each case and can remove the badge at any time. Verified contributors still have to follow every rule here.

Copyright

Respect photographers. If someone has used your photo without permission, follow the notice process in section 11 of the Terms of Service.

Reporting problems and appeals

Use the Report button on a place or comment, or email our contact email. If you think we made a mistake removing your content or limiting your account, email us with the link and your reason and we will take another look.
