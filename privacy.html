Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data TerraLens collects when you use the site, how we use it and the choices you have.

1. Who we are

TerraLens is operated by Randy Blaze (the "Operator"), who is responsible for your personal data as described here. Contact: our contact email.

2. What we collect

  • Account data: when you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google. We never see your Google password.
  • Content you create: places and their details, photos, coordinates you enter, comments, reports, likes, saved places, collections and trips.
  • AI messages: text you send to the AI assistant or trip planner.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser and device type, pages requested and error information, recorded automatically in server logs by our hosting and database providers.
  • Session data: a sign-in token stored in your browser so you stay signed in.

3. How we use your data and why

  • To provide the service: create your account and profile, show your places and comments, keep your saves and collections (contract).
  • To keep the community safe: review content, handle reports, apply rate limits and prevent spam and abuse (legitimate interests).
  • To send you service messages and answer your requests (contract and legitimate interests).
  • To power AI features when you choose to use them (contract).
  • To meet legal obligations such as handling copyright notices (legal obligation).

4. What other people can see

Public: your display name, your verified badge if you have one, the places you upload and their photos, and your comments. Your email address is not shown to other users. The number of likes a photo has is public, but who liked it is not. Your saved places and trips are private to you.

5. Photos and location

Photos are resized in your browser before upload, which removes hidden metadata such as GPS location and camera details. The coordinates you type into the upload form are published with the place. Please do not publish the exact location of private homes or of sensitive wildlife or heritage sites.

6. Who we share data with

We use these service providers, who process data for us:

  • Supabase for the database, sign-in and file storage.
  • Vercel for hosting and server functions, which also records request logs.
  • Google for sign-in, and for fonts loaded from Google Fonts (which shows your IP address to Google).
  • Unsplash, whose images are loaded from its servers and whose search we use to find photos.
  • OpenStreetMap, whose embedded map loads from its servers (your IP address is visible to it).
  • OpenRouter and the AI model provider it routes to, which receive the text you send to the AI assistant.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for third-party advertising. We may disclose data if the law requires it or to protect people from harm.

7. Cookies and similar technologies

TerraLens does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser stores a sign-in token in local storage, which is strictly necessary to keep you signed in, and a few temporary values such as where to send you after sign-in. If we add analytics later we will use a privacy-friendly tool and update this page. Third-party content such as maps and fonts may set their own technical data under their own policies.

8. The AI assistant

Messages you send to the AI assistant and trip planner are sent through OpenRouter to an AI model provider to generate a reply. We send your message text, not your name or email, but you should not include personal or sensitive information in your messages. Replies can be wrong.

9. How long we keep data

We keep your account and content until you delete it or ask us to. Server logs are kept by our providers for a limited time. Reports and moderation records may be kept longer to protect the community and handle disputes. Deleted data can remain in encrypted backups for a limited period before it is overwritten.

10. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, receive a copy, object to or restrict some processing, and withdraw consent. To use these rights, or to delete your account and content, email our contact email. We may need to confirm it is you. You can also delete your own places and comments in the site. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data you can complain to your local data protection authority.

11. Children

TerraLens is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

12. International transfers

Our providers operate in several countries, including the United States and the European Union, so your data may be processed outside the country where you live. We choose providers that protect data with appropriate safeguards.

13. Security

We use encrypted connections, access rules in the database so that people can only change their own data, rate limits and moderation tools. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

14. Changes

We may update this policy. If the changes are material we will tell you on the site. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.

15. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: our contact email.
