Write for TerraLens

Guest posts, US$5 one-time fee

The TerraLens blog is written by our own team. Only TerraLens admins can publish articles. If you have a travel, destination or photography story you would like to share with our readers, you can publish a guest post by emailing us and paying a one-time fee of US$5.

How it works

  1. Email us your idea: a working title, a short outline, and links to anything you have written or photographed before.
  2. We reply to say whether the idea fits. If it does, we send you the payment details for the US$5 fee.
  3. You send the article after paying. Aim for 500 to 1,500 words, with any photos you own.
  4. We review and publish. We may edit lightly for clarity and format, then publish it with a clear "Guest post" label and your name.

Email us your idea

What the fee covers

The US$5 covers our editing, formatting and hosting time. It does not buy a guaranteed ranking, traffic or any particular result. If we decide not to publish your article after you have paid, we refund the fee.

What we publish

  • Original writing about places, travel planning, destinations or photography. No copied or mass-produced text.
  • Practical, honest and useful for readers. Photos must be yours or properly licensed.
  • No gambling, adult, illegal or misleading content, and nothing that breaks our Content Policy.
  • Up to two links to your own site or work. Links in guest posts are marked as sponsored (rel="sponsored nofollow"), so they do not pass search ranking credit.

Labelling and rights

Every guest post is clearly labelled as a guest post, with a short note that the author paid a fee to publish it. You keep ownership of your writing and give TerraLens permission to publish it, resize your photos and promote the article. We can edit, decline or remove a post at any time, for example if it turns out to break the rules. See the Terms of Service for the full details.
